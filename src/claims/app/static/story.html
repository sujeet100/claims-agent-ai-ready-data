Motor insurance in plain words

What happens to a car-accident claim

🚗

An accident

A car is damaged in a collision on 12 October.

📝

A claim is filed

The owner asks the insurer for ₹4.2 lakh to repair it.

📜

Check the policy

Was the car covered, and for what, on the day of the accident?

📄

Read the evidence

The police report (FIR), the garage's invoice, the surveyor's report.

⚖️

Decide

Pay quickly ("fast-track"), or send it to investigators.
